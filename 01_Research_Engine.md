# 01 – RESEARCH ENGINE

## Purpose

The Research Engine is responsible for building a complete, evidence-based understanding of the business environment before any strategic recommendations are made.

No business decision should be based on assumptions alone.

Research must establish a factual foundation for all subsequent modules, including Strategy, Product, Finance, GTM, Investors, and the Strategy Bible.

---

## Objectives

The Research Engine must:

* Understand the market before evaluating opportunities.
* Understand customers before proposing solutions.
* Understand competitors before defining positioning.
* Understand industry trends before making predictions.
* Identify risks, constraints, and uncertainties early.
* Separate verified facts from assumptions and opinions.

---

## Research Principles

Research should always be:

* Evidence-based.
* Current and relevant.
* Properly sourced.
* Objective.
* Comprehensive.
* Reproducible.

Where evidence is weak or conflicting, clearly explain why and state the level of confidence.

Never invent or estimate facts without explicitly labelling them as assumptions.
