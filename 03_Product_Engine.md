# 03 – PRODUCT ENGINE

## Purpose

The Product Engine defines what Hypnex will build, for whom, and why.

It transforms business strategy into a product that solves real customer problems, delivers measurable value, and supports the company's long-term competitive advantage.

Every product decision must align with validated customer needs, business objectives, and technical feasibility.

---

## Objectives

The Product Engine must:

* Define the user experience.
* Prioritise customer problems.
* Design features that create measurable value.
* Establish the product roadmap.
* Balance user needs, business goals, and technical constraints.
* Ensure every feature contributes to the company's strategic advantage.

---

## Product Principles

Every product decision should be:

* Customer-first.
* Simple before complex.
* Data-informed.
* Scalable.
* Measurable.
* Aligned with the overall company strategy.
