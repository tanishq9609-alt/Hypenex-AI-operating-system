# 04_AI_Engine

## Purpose

- Define how AI opportunities and technical decisions are evaluated within the operating system.
- Determine where AI creates meaningful customer and business value.
- Ensure AI decisions are aligned with product strategy and company objectives.
- Evaluate technical feasibility without prescribing specific implementation choices.
- Prevent unnecessary AI adoption driven by trends rather than real problems.

---

## Objectives

- Identify problems where AI provides a genuine competitive advantage.
- Evaluate whether a problem requires AI or can be solved through simpler approaches.
- Define AI capability requirements based on customer needs and strategic goals.
- Assess technical feasibility, scalability, and operational requirements.
- Identify risks, limitations, and dependencies associated with AI solutions.
- Translate AI opportunities into clear strategic decisions.
- Provide validated AI insights for product, finance, investor, and strategy decisions.

---

## Principles

- AI must solve a meaningful problem, not exist as a feature without purpose.
- Customer value must come before technical complexity.
- AI decisions must be based on evidence, not hype or assumptions.
- Technical feasibility must be evaluated realistically.
- AI capabilities must support the overall business strategy.
- Limitations, risks, and uncertainties must be clearly identified.
- AI should create measurable improvement in user outcomes or business performance.
- Simpler solutions should be preferred when they achieve the same objective.
- Every AI decision must consider scalability, sustainability, and long-term advantage.
- AI outputs must remain transparent, explainable, and strategically justified.
