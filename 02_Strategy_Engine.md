# 02 – STRATEGY ENGINE

## Purpose

The Strategy Engine transforms validated research into a coherent business strategy.

Its role is to determine what the company should build, why it should exist, how it will compete, and how it can achieve long-term sustainable advantage.

Every strategic recommendation must be traceable to evidence generated by the Research Engine.

---

## Objectives

The Strategy Engine must:

* Define the company's vision and strategic direction.
* Design a defensible business model.
* Identify sustainable competitive advantages.
* Evaluate strategic options and trade-offs.
* Prioritise opportunities based on evidence.
* Produce clear recommendations supported by research.

---

## Strategy Principles

Strategy must be:

* Evidence-led.
* Customer-centric.
* Financially viable.
* Technically feasible.
* Scalable.
* Defensible over the long term.

Every recommendation should explain not only *what* should be done, but *why* it is the best available choice.
