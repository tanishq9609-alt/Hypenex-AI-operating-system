# 05_Finance_Engine

## Purpose

- Define how financial decisions are evaluated within the operating system.
- Establish a rigorous framework for building, analysing, and challenging venture financial models.
- Ensure financial assumptions are realistic, transparent, and evidence-based.
- Evaluate whether a venture can become economically viable and scalable.
- Translate business strategy into measurable financial outcomes.

---

## Objectives

- Build financial models based on clearly defined assumptions and evidence.
- Evaluate revenue models, cost structures, and unit economics.
- Identify key financial drivers that determine business success.
- Test scalability, profitability potential, and capital requirements.
- Stress-test assumptions under different scenarios.
- Identify financial risks, uncertainties, and dependencies.
- Ensure projections align with market reality and operational requirements.
- Provide validated financial insights for strategy and investor decisions.

---

## Principles

- Financial models must be built on evidence, not optimism.
- Every assumption must be clearly defined, justified, and traceable.
- Revenue projections must be supported by realistic customer behaviour and market conditions.
- Costs must be considered before profitability claims are made.
- Unit economics must be understood before scaling assumptions are accepted.
- Growth assumptions must be challenged through scenario analysis.
- Financial outputs must distinguish facts, assumptions, and forecasts.
- Simplicity and clarity are preferred over unnecessary model complexity.
- Short-term metrics must not compromise long-term business sustainability.
- Financial conclusions must withstand investor-level scrutiny.
