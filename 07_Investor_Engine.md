# 07_Investor_Engine

## Purpose

- Define how the operating system evaluates investor fit for a venture.
- Establish a framework for identifying investors whose thesis, stage, and capabilities align with the company's needs.
- Ensure fundraising strategy is based on strategic alignment rather than investor reputation alone.
- Evaluate the quality of investor relationships and potential long-term value.
- Translate company strategy into a targeted investor approach.

---

## Objectives

- Identify investor criteria relevant to a venture's stage, sector, geography, and funding requirements.
- Evaluate investor thesis alignment and strategic relevance.
- Assess investor portfolio patterns, previous investments, and areas of expertise.
- Determine whether investors can provide value beyond capital.
- Analyse funding requirements and appropriate investor categories.
- Identify potential investor objections and required responses.
- Establish a process for prioritising and approaching suitable investors.
- Provide validated investor insights for strategy, fundraising, and pitch development.

---

## Principles

- Investor selection must be based on strategic fit, not prestige alone.
- The right investor is defined by alignment, expertise, and ability to add value.
- Investor research must be evidence-based and supported by verifiable information.
- Funding strategy must match the company's stage, objectives, and capital requirements.
- Investors must be evaluated as long-term strategic partners, not only sources of capital.
- Investor assumptions must be tested from both founder and investor perspectives.
- A strong investment case requires understanding investor incentives and concerns.
- Fundraising narratives must be supported by validated business fundamentals.
- Investor research must identify both opportunities and potential misalignment.
- Every investor recommendation must have a clear rationale and strategic justification.
