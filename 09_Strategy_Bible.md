# 09_Strategy_Bible

## Purpose

- Define the structure for compiling validated venture intelligence into a single strategic source of truth.
- Create a consistent framework for transforming outputs from all operating system engines into a coherent company strategy.
- Ensure strategic decisions are based on evidence, validated assumptions, and Red Team review.
- Provide the foundation from which investor materials, operating decisions, and future strategy are created.
- Act as the final reference document for understanding a venture's opportunity, strategy, and execution plan.

---

## Objectives

- Compile validated insights from all completed engines into one structured strategic document.
- Establish a complete view of the company's purpose, market, product, business model, and growth strategy.
- Ensure every strategic conclusion has passed adversarial review through the Red Team Engine.
- Separate confirmed evidence, assumptions, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
- Create a document that can be understood by founders, operators, investors, and strategic partners.
- Provide the validated foundation for the Pitch Deck Engine and future decision-making.

---

## Principles

- The Strategy Bible must contain only validated strategic conclusions.
- Every section must identify the evidence and reasoning behind its conclusions.
- No assumption should be presented as a confirmed fact.
- All major claims must have survived Red Team Engine review before inclusion.
- Contradictions between engines must be resolved before finalisation.
- Unknowns must be clearly documented rather than hidden.
- Strategic clarity is prioritised over excessive detail.
- The Strategy Bible is a living document that evolves as new evidence emerges.
- The Strategy Bible must represent the strongest defensible version of the venture.

---

# Strategy Bible Template Structure

## 1. Company Summary

### Purpose

Defines the fundamental identity and strategic purpose of the venture.

### Must Contain

- Company mission.
- Core problem being solved.
- Solution overview.
- Target customer.
- Unique value proposition.
- Why the company exists now.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- The problem is real and significant.
- The solution addresses a meaningful customer need.
- The company purpose is clearly differentiated.
- The timing and market conditions justify the opportunity.

---

## 2. Market Opportunity

### Purpose

Defines the size, characteristics, and attractiveness of the market.

### Must Contain

- Target market definition.
- Market size analysis.
- Market growth trends.
- Customer demand evidence.
- Industry dynamics.
- Relevant market opportunities and constraints.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Market estimates are evidence-based.
- Market size is not exaggerated.
- Growth assumptions are realistic.
- The opportunity exists beyond optimistic projections.

---

## 3. Customer Understanding

### Purpose

Defines who the venture serves and why customers will adopt the solution.

### Must Contain

- Ideal customer profile.
- Customer pain points.
- Customer motivations.
- Buying behaviour.
- Adoption barriers.
- Customer validation evidence.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- The customer problem is genuine.
- Customers have sufficient motivation to act.
- Adoption assumptions are realistic.
- Evidence supports customer demand.

---

## 4. Competitive Positioning

### Purpose

Defines how the venture differentiates itself within the market.

### Must Contain

- Competitive landscape.
- Direct and indirect competitors.
- Current alternatives.
- Differentiation strategy.
- Defensible advantages.
- Long-term positioning.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Competitors have been accurately analysed.
- Differentiation is meaningful.
- Claimed advantages are defensible.
- The venture is not ignoring existing solutions.

---

## 5. Product Strategy

### Purpose

Defines how the product creates customer value and evolves over time.

### Must Contain

- Product vision.
- Core capabilities.
- User experience principles.
- Product roadmap logic.
- Customer value creation.
- Key product assumptions.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Product solves the identified problem.
- Features are prioritised correctly.
- Product complexity is justified.
- Customer value exceeds implementation effort.

---

## 6. AI and Technical Strategy

### Purpose

Defines how technology and AI contribute to competitive advantage.

### Must Contain

- AI opportunities.
- Technical capabilities required.
- Automation opportunities.
- Technical limitations.
- Scalability considerations.
- Technology-related risks.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- AI claims are realistic.
- Technology creates meaningful advantage.
- Technical assumptions are achievable.
- AI is solving a real business problem.

---

## 7. Business Model Logic

### Purpose

Defines how the venture creates, delivers, and captures value.

### Must Contain

- Revenue model.
- Pricing logic.
- Customer value exchange.
- Cost structure.
- Scaling mechanism.
- Business model assumptions.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Revenue assumptions are realistic.
- Customers have willingness to pay.
- The business model can scale.
- The economics are logically consistent.

---

## 8. Unit Economics Summary

### Purpose

Defines the financial mechanics that determine business sustainability.

### Must Contain

- Customer acquisition assumptions.
- Customer lifetime value assumptions.
- Gross margin logic.
- Payback considerations.
- Cost drivers.
- Financial risks.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Financial assumptions are evidence-based.
- Growth expectations are realistic.
- Key metrics are achievable.
- Weak economic assumptions are addressed.

---

## 9. Go-To-Market Approach

### Purpose

Defines how the venture reaches customers and creates scalable growth.

### Must Contain

- Target customer segments.
- Positioning.
- Acquisition channels.
- Sales approach.
- Growth strategy.
- Market entry sequence.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Customer acquisition assumptions are realistic.
- Channels are appropriate.
- Growth strategy can scale.
- Distribution challenges have been considered.

---

## 10. Operating Strategy

### Purpose

Defines the capabilities required to execute the company strategy.

### Must Contain

- Key operational requirements.
- Required capabilities.
- Strategic priorities.
- Execution milestones.
- Resource requirements.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Execution requirements are achievable.
- Resource assumptions are realistic.
- Priorities are correctly sequenced.

---

## 11. Key Risks and Open Questions

### Purpose

Defines unresolved challenges requiring monitoring or validation.

### Must Contain

- Strategic risks.
- Market risks.
- Product risks.
- Technical risks.
- Financial risks.
- Investor concerns.
- Outstanding assumptions.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Major risks have been identified.
- Unknowns are clearly documented.
- Validation priorities are defined.

---

## 12. Investor Strategy

### Purpose

Defines the investment case and suitable investor profile.

### Must Contain

- Investment thesis.
- Funding requirements.
- Appropriate investor characteristics.
- Target investor categories.
- Strategic investor value.
- Fundraising narrative.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Investment case is defensible.
- Investor fit is strategically justified.
- Funding requirements are realistic.
- Major investor objections have been addressed.

---

## 13. Target Investor List

### Purpose

Defines potential investors that match the venture's requirements.

### Must Contain

- Investor name.
- Investment thesis.
- Relevant portfolio examples.
- Stage preference.
- Geographic preference.
- Strategic relevance.

### Red Team Validation Required

Before completion, Red Team must confirm:

- Investors genuinely match the venture.
- Recommendations are evidence-based.
- Investor alignment is not based only on reputation.

---

# Final Validation Requirement

The Strategy Bible can only be considered complete when:

- All engine outputs have been reviewed.
- All major claims have survived Red Team analysis.
- Unsupported assumptions have been removed or labelled.
- Strategic contradictions have been resolved.
- The final document represents the strongest evidence-backed version of the venture.

The Strategy Bible is the approved foundation for the Pitch Deck Engine and all future strategic decisions.
