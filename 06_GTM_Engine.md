# 06_GTM_Engine

## Purpose

- Define how the operating system evaluates and designs go-to-market strategies for a venture.
- Establish a framework for selecting, testing, and scaling customer acquisition channels.
- Ensure market entry decisions are based on evidence rather than assumptions.
- Determine how a venture creates repeatable and scalable customer growth.
- Translate product value into a clear customer acquisition strategy.

---

## Objectives

- Identify the ideal customer segments and their purchasing behaviours.
- Evaluate and prioritise potential acquisition channels.
- Define the sequence of market entry activities.
- Develop strategies for customer acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion.
- Establish measurable indicators for validating a go-to-market motion.
- Test assumptions before committing significant resources.
- Evaluate channel efficiency, scalability, and long-term sustainability.
- Provide validated GTM insights for strategy, finance, investor, and pitch decisions.

---

## Principles

- Customer understanding must come before channel selection.
- Go-to-market decisions must be based on evidence and validated learning.
- The smallest effective market entry strategy should be tested before scaling.
- Channels must be evaluated based on measurable outcomes, not popularity or trends.
- Distribution advantage is as important as product quality.
- Growth assumptions must be challenged before significant investment is made.
- Early traction should be demonstrated before aggressive scaling.
- Customer acquisition costs must be considered alongside customer value.
- GTM strategies must align with the product, business model, and available resources.
- Every GTM decision must define how success will be measured and validated.
